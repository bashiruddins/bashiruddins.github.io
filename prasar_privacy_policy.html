Privacy Policy

We use persistent cookies which are permanently placed on your computer to store non-personal and profiling information. While cookies have unique identification nos, personal information (name, a/c no, contact nos etc) SHALL NOT be stored on the cookies.

Information We Collect

We collect certain information from you to provide and improve our services. This includes:

  • Phone Number: Your phone number is collected to facilitate account creation, authentication, and communication.
